Caliptra: runtime work and waiting

This view shows call-stack samples of the initial static placement, before the revised worker map. Each bar is one worker's sampled cycle distribution. It is not a wall-clock timeline or a profile of the separate serial build.

Worker 0 is the caller. Workers 0–3 and 4–7 occupy separate chiplets on one socket. ACT executes on the caller; selected NBA units can move between workers. Library copies are attributed using their observed call stacks. Hover over a bar segment for its sampled percentage.
